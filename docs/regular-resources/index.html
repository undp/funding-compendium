    Lion’s share goes to programmes

    The lion’s share of the core programme budget for 2022–2025 is channelled through country programmes.

    Strong support for least developed and low-income countries

    Over 80 percent of core resources are allocated to least developed countries where development needs are greatest, and 78 percent to low-income countries.

    Africa receives the largest share

    In line with development needs, the largest share of core resources is allocated to Africa, followed by Asia and the Arab States.

    The institutional budget enables programmatic impact

    UNDP’s institutional budget supports investments in institutional capacity, development effectiveness, the maintenance of the highest levels of independent oversight, transparency and accountability, and special-purpose initiatives (UNV and UNCDF).

    Government contributions to local office costs

    Annual government contributions to local office costs (GLOC) are an important source of revenue and take the form of cash or in-kind contributions from host governments. In low- and middle-income countries, $39 million was received in 2025 (up from $29 million in 2024), consisting of $31 million in cash contributions and $8 million of in-kind contributions for premises and utilities.
