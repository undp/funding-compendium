Annual contributions increased to $5 billion in 2025, up by 2 percent from $4.9 billion in 2024.

1 In 2019, UNDP refined its accounting policy on IPSAS 23 (non-exchange) revenues. Under this policy, UNDP records the full value of funding agreements when they are signed, even when cash has not been received for the majority of contribution agreements. Any uncollected cash associated with funding agreements is held as a receivable. Under UNDP financial regulations and rules, UNDP is permitted to spend only up to the amount of cash received. In this document, where appliable, “annual contributions” are therefore aligned with past revenue recognition policies for contributions (i.e., cash received in a reporting year, plus receivables due in a reporting year).

Annual contributions to regular resources decreased to $442 million, down by 24 percent from $581 million in 2024.
Their share of total annual contributions decreased to 9 percent from 12 percent in 2024 and remains far below the Funding Compact target of 30 percent.
Multi-year commitments amounted to $165 million, or 37 percent of regular resources contributions, down from 40 percent in 2024.

Annual contributions to other resources increased to $4.6 billion, up by 6 percent from $4.3 billion in 2024.

{{homepage.transparency.headline}}


A long-standing commitment to transparency

As a member of the International Aid Transparency Initiative (IATI) since its creation in 2008, UNDP has led outreach efforts with partner countries and UN agencies to champion the aid transparency standard and make it relevant for national development planning, public financial management and mutual accountability of aid, development and humanitarian funding. UNDP ranked sixth overall in the 2024 Aid Transparency Index and was the second most transparent UN agency.

UNDP’s Transparency Portal allows open, comprehensive public access to data on over 4,000 UNDP projects, with links to profile pages for every donor. The portal also shows UNDP’s contribution to the SDGs and offers an online training platform on the IATI Standard.



Promoting transparency across the UN system

UNDP actively participated in defining and implementing a set of UN data standards for system-wide financial reporting.

Audit reports

UNDP has been publishing audit reports issued by the Office of Audit and Investigations since 2012, and has obtained its 19th consecutive unqualified (clean) audit from the UN Board of Auditors.

Regular and other resources, 2025


Annual contributions increased to $5 billion in 2025,
up by 2 percent from $4.9 billion in 2024.

Annual contributions to regular resources decreased to $442 million,
down by 24 percent from $581 million in 2024.

Annual contributions to other resources increased to $4.6 billion,
up by 6 percent from $4.3 billion in 2024.



Regular resources are critical to UNDP's ability to prioritize countries' needs, respond swiftly in crises and maintain high standards of accountability and transparency. They also allow UNDP to sustain country presence and upstream policy expertise that other types of funding cannot support. Regular resources also carry strong catalytic value, mobilizing additional resources for development. Insufficient regular resources would therefore severely compromise UNDP's unique ability to respond rapidly to emergencies and address critical needs that other funding sources do not cover. The continued decline in regular resources also has serious implications for UNDP's ability to deliver the intended results of the Strategic Plan, restore and accelerate progress towards the SDGs and maintain robust oversight and accountability systems.

Trends in regular and other resources, 2022–2025
UNDP maintained broadly stable annual contributions throughout the period covered by the 2022–2025 Strategic Plan, averaging approximately $4.9 billion per year. Annual contributions reached a peak of $5.0 billion in both 2023 and 2025.
