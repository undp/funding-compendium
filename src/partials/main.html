2025 Financial Highlights

24% decrease in core funding

(regular resources)
Core contributions received in 2025 decreased to $442 million, from $581 million in 2024.

14% decrease in funding windows

Contributions from thematic funding windows decreased to $115 million in 2025, down from $133 million in 2024.

17% increase in government development financing

Contributions from government financing increased to $1.4 billion in 2025, up from $1.2 billion in 2024 and representing 28% of total resources.
