Government contributions to local office costs

Annual government contributions to local office costs (GLOC) are an important source of revenue and take the form of cash or in-kind contributions from host governments. In low- and middle-income countries, $39 million was received in 2025 ($29 million in 2024), consisting of $31 million in cash contributions and $8 million of in-kind contributions for premises and utilities.
